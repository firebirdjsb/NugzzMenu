# Original NugzzMenu V-twin audio

These are original authored engine loops, generated by
`scripts/Generate-MotorcycleAudio.py`. No downloaded recordings, game recordings
or third-party sound-library samples are included in the active sound bank.

The design follows the requested 315-degree/405-degree firing rhythm over a
720-degree four-stroke cycle. Bipolar exhaust-pressure pulses, damped pipe modes,
small combustion-strength variations and colored transient noise create a dry,
deep idle and a sharper loaded exhaust voice. No outdoor ambience is baked in.

Mono signed PCM16 at 24 kHz; six seconds per bank. Idle bank: 1200 RPM; loaded bank:
2400 RPM. Runtime pitch uses the same simulated RPM for both, preserving the
firing cadence through their blend. Playback idles at 1000 RPM and caps at 3000.
See design.json for hashes and measured bounds. This is an authored game sound,
not a recording or a calibrated physical reproduction of a specific motorcycle.
